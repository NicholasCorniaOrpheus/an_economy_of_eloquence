<font size="6"> _An Economy of Eloquence_ is a game about **discovery**, **plurality**, and **unexpected journeys** designed for researchers and librarians.</font>

This collaboratative research tool in the form of a board game is still in development by [Nicholas Cornia](https://orpheusinstituut.be/en/research/researchers/nicholas-cornia) and the [Resounding Libraries](https://orpheusinstituut.be/en/projects/resounding-libraries) cluster at Orpheus Instituut.
